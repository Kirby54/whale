## What's new in 0.5.0

### 📉 Analysis becomes a personal market terminal
- **Command bar, Bloomberg style:** `TSLA GP` (chart), `AAPL DES` (company), `NVDA N` (news), `ECO` (macro), `EQS` (screener), `CORR` (correlation), `GRID`, `W` (watchlist), `BTC ALRT 70000` (price alert). Also in the Ctrl+K palette.
- **Chart:** candles by default (hollow when the price rises, filled when it falls), OHLC bars, area or line, indicators menu, O/H/L/C under the mouse.
- **Editable drawings:** levels, trend lines, Fibonacci, long and short positions. Select, drag, name, color, set an alert on a level, delete.
- **Watchlist and price alerts**, checked every minute while Whale is open.
- **Company:** results, margins, valuation, balance sheet and cash flow of US-listed companies, from their SEC filings.
- **News, Macro** (yield curve, VIX, dollar, commodities, US economy with a free FRED key), **Screener**, **Correlation matrix** and a **Grid** of charts.
- **Data sources:** free by default (Yahoo Finance, Binance, ECB, SEC EDGAR, Google News); add your own FRED, Polygon.io or Finnhub key. Keys stay in the Windows Credential Manager.

### ✨ Apple Liquid Glass design
- Pure black base, frosted glass panels and menus, a liquid lens over the brain, and a light that follows the mouse along the glass edges.
- Every color comes from one set of design tokens; solid surfaces when Windows asks for reduced transparency.

### 🐋 Brain
- 40,000 particles stream around the whale like water, and the whale has more points.
